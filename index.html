02 / Explore real places

From the map to your shortlist.

Look up real OpenStreetMap places near the selected city centre. These are map records, not verified ratings, opening hours, recommendations, or booking availability.

Choose a category and press “Find real places”. This makes one small, on-demand query; results may be limited if the public service is busy.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors · Overpass API. Public services are community-run and may be rate-limited. Verify details with the venue before visiting.
